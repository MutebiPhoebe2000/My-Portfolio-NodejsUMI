About
Me.

Hi, I'm Phoebe — a developer, artist, and lifelong learner based in Kampala, Uganda. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Counseling Psychology and I am currently pursuing a Postgraduate Diploma in Information Technology at Uganda Management Institute, deepening my knowledge in computing systems, networking, databases, and ICT management.

I trained at Refactory Academy where I studied the foundations of software development, covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub, GitLab, and object-oriented development. I have since built websites, web applications, and e-commerce platforms using WordPress, Webflow, WooCommerce, Elementor, Pantheon, and Node.js. I am currently expanding my skills by learning React.

Beyond coding, I am also a passionate artist who loves to draw and create. Art has always shaped the way I think about design, colour, and user experience. I believe that great websites are not just functional — they are also beautiful, and I carry that belief into every project I build.
